# Licensing

Nuka Physics is **dual-licensed**. You may use it under **either**:

1. the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0** (open source), **or**
2. a **commercial license** from the author (for closed-source / proprietary use).

You choose the one that fits your use.

## 1. Open source — AGPL-3.0

Nuka Physics is free software under the [GNU AGPL v3.0](LICENSE). You may use,
study, modify, and redistribute it under the AGPL's terms. In particular, the
AGPL requires that **if you distribute Nuka (modified or not), or make it
available to users over a network as part of a service, you must release the
complete corresponding source of your work under the AGPL** — including your own
code that is combined with Nuka.

This option is ideal for open-source projects, academic research, evaluation, and
personal use.

## 2. Commercial license — keep your source closed

If the AGPL's copyleft does not fit your product — for example, you want to embed
Nuka Physics in a **closed-source product** or offer it as part of a **proprietary
or SaaS service without releasing your source** — you can obtain a **commercial
license** that waives the AGPL obligations.

The commercial license is granted by the author in exchange for **sponsorship**.
This is what funds continued development of Nuka.

**To obtain a commercial license**, contact the author / copyright holder:

- open an issue on the project repository, or
- email the maintainer: **nidhogxt@outlook.com**

State your intended use and we will arrange a license.

## Why dual licensing

The author holds the copyright to Nuka Physics and offers it to the community
under the AGPL, while funding development through commercial licenses for users
who need to keep their own source closed. This is the same model used by projects
such as Qt and (historically) MongoDB.

## Third-party components

Components shipped with Nuka (e.g. Dear ImGui — MIT, V-HACD — BSD; see
[NOTICE](NOTICE)) remain under their own permissive licenses, which are
compatible with **both** the AGPL and a commercial license. The NVIDIA CUDA
Toolkit remains under the NVIDIA SLA and is not relicensed by this project.

## Note on the public history

Earlier releases of Nuka Physics were published under the Apache License 2.0.
Those already-released versions remain available under Apache-2.0; this dual
AGPL/commercial model applies to the current and future source.
